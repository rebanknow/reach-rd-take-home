# Reach Industries — R&D upsell exercise

Reach is already a Caribou transfer-pricing customer. We want to decide whether to offer them our UK R&D claim service, using the information we already have.

## Your task

**Should Caribou pursue this R&D upsell, and how should we make that decision for other customers?**

Design a repeatable assessment process and apply it to Reach. Decide what information matters, what is missing and how to obtain it. Explain what our software, the client and our tax expert should each do—and when.

Caribou's starting fee is **15% of the customer's actual tax saving or refund**, invoiced after the client receives the payout. We may negotiate **10% or 20%** depending on the opportunity. Assess the commercial case and what we need to establish before quoting. Make any missing inputs or assumptions explicit.

## What to deliver

1. **Your proposed approach:** the process, the information it uses or requests, and the division of work between software, client and expert. Explain your key choices.
2. **A worked Reach assessment:** an estimated UK customer-benefit range, Caribou's potential fee at 10%, 15% and 20%, and your recommendation on whether to pursue the opportunity. Show the calculations, assumptions and uncertainties behind it.
3. **Something we can inspect:** a spreadsheet, prompt, script, diagram, written walkthrough or another format that makes your approach concrete. These can all be part of one submission.

Spend **no more than three hours**, including research. **Working code is optional; visual polish is not assessed.** We care about how you turn expert-led work into a practical, repeatable product process. You are not expected to prepare a complete R&D claim. A conditional recommendation is fine if you explain what would resolve it.

Share your files or a link before the interview. In the **first 30 minutes**, we will discuss your approach and assessment. In the **next 30 minutes**, we will work together in code to implement a small part of your approach and try it on another supplied case. You may use your usual coding and AI tools.
