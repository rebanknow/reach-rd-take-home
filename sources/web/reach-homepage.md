# Augmenting humanity, one industry at a time

We’re here to transform the way entire industries operate. Not by automating people out, but by freeing them to focus on the work only humans can do.

At Reach Industries, we build frontier technologies and design systems that put humans and collaboration at the center.

## Our First Step? Science.

Since the discovery of fire, science has illuminated humanity’s path forward. Science is immensely valuable, but it's complex and heavily regulated - precisely why we chose it as our starting point. Laboratories drive breakthroughs in medicine, energy, and materials, yet labs still rely on human eyes and manual record-keeping for many critical tasks.
