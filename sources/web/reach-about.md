## Silas Adekunle

Co-founder, CEO

As CEO, Silas leads the company’s mission to build the visual intelligence layer for science, drawing on a deep background in frontier technologies. Silas has built and scaled globally distributed products at the intersection of robotics, AR, and cloud systems. Named Forbes 30 Under 30 in both Europe and Africa, he brings a unique blend of technical vision, entrepreneurial drive, and a global perspective to the life sciences space.

## Chris Beck

Co-founder, CTO

As CTO, Chris leads the development of Reach Industries’ AI and technology infrastructure, drawing on 15+ years of experience in computer vision, robotics, and embedded systems. He leads engineering and product development and has built and scaled multidisciplinary engineering teams that deliver complex hardware-software systems globally.
